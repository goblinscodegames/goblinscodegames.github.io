About this policy

This policy covers the Goblin’s Code Games Studio website at goblinscodegames.github.io and correspondence sent to the studio. It does not cover any game or application. A game-specific policy will be linked separately when applicable.

Last updated: November 12, 2026.

Who is responsible

Privacy enquiries: greencode.gamedev@gmail.com.

Hosting and technical logs

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Your browser requests pages, fonts, and artwork from that hosting service. GitHub states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in.

GitHub handles its service data under its own policies. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement for information about data categories, retention, transfers, and provider rights requests. The studio does not control GitHub’s security-log retention.

Email communication

If you email us, we receive your email address, the content of your message, and any name, attachments, or other information you choose to include. Providing this information is voluntary, but we need a reply address and enough context to respond.

We use correspondence to answer enquiries and manage the resulting conversation, based on our legitimate interest in communicating with people who contact the studio (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Where you request steps towards a contract, Article 6(1)(b) may apply instead. Records required by law are handled under Article 6(1)(c).

Messages go to a Gmail address and are handled through Google’s email service. Your own email provider also processes delivery data. See the Google Privacy Policy for its service processing.

Correspondence is retained for as long as needed to resolve the enquiry and manage related matters, including any applicable legal recordkeeping or claim periods. The appropriate period depends on the subject of the exchange.

Cookies and tracking

The website’s own code does not set cookies, use local storage, run analytics or advertising trackers, or create visitor accounts. Fonts and artwork are served locally with the website. There are no embedded social feeds, videos, or third-party forms.

The optional “Light the Forge” interaction changes the illustration on your device. It sends no data and does not store your choice. Hosting still involves the technical processing described above.

Providers and external links

Hosting and email providers handle the data needed to provide their services. Information may also be disclosed where legally required. Provider services may involve processing outside the European Economic Area; their privacy notices explain applicable transfer arrangements. This policy does not promise that all processing stays in Poland or the EEA.

External links take you to services with their own privacy practices. Those pages are not loaded merely because a link appears here. Selecting an email link opens your mail application; it does not send a message automatically.

Your privacy rights

Subject to the conditions in the GDPR, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction, and portability of your personal data. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. If processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.

To exercise rights concerning studio correspondence, email greencode.gamedev@gmail.com. We may need proportionate information to verify your identity. For data GitHub or Google controls independently, use their privacy request channels.

You can complain to a competent supervisory authority, including Poland’s President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO). No automated decisions or profiling are performed by this website’s code.

Changes to this policy

If the website’s features or processing change, this policy will be updated and the date above revised. Any future game policy will describe that game’s actual features and services separately.
